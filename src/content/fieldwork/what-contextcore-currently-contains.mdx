## What ContextCore Currently Contains
ContextCore is the first part of the Inspiral agentic flow. It turns accepted source material into governed capability knowledge that later stages can reuse without rediscovering meaning from raw documents.
The important outcome is not simply that information has been extracted or displayed. The outcome is that meaning, provenance, relationships, evidence, boundaries and current state remain connected.
<aside class="article-callout"><strong>DIRECT ANSWER</strong><p>ContextCore currently contains a completed, governed source-to-knowledge foundation for the current Inspiral scope. It preserves where material came from, what reusable meaning was extracted, how accepted meaning is connected, why capability membership is valid and what remains uncertain.</p></aside>
## The question
What must happen before source material can be safely reused by later stages of an agentic system?
A source document is not yet usable context. Extraction alone can produce plausible text, but it does not establish authority, provenance, relationship validity or capability membership.
ContextCore addresses that gap by making those conditions explicit before later reasoning begins.
## The ContextCore flow
The ContextCore part of the Inspiral agentic flow is:
<ol class="contextcore-flow" aria-label="ContextCore stages in the Inspiral agentic flow">
  <li><span>STG-01</span><strong>Source &amp; Signal Intake</strong></li>
  <li><span>STG-02</span><strong>Semantic Modelling</strong></li>
  <li><span>STG-03</span><strong>Semantic Relationships</strong></li>
  <li><span>STG-04</span><strong>Capability Baselines</strong></li>
</ol>
Each handoff narrows uncertainty while preserving the evidence and authority needed by the next stage.

Source type is only one routing input; source class, semantic scope, extraction layers, eligible stage and authority/use scope determine how each source may be used.
STG-01 freezes the current source denominator, retrieves selected sources, preserves their controlled identity and provenance, and creates bounded package or chunk materialisations. It also records excluded, unavailable or review-required sources instead of silently dropping them.
This creates a reliable starting boundary:
> The system knows which source was selected, which version was processed, what route it was permitted to take and whether the package is complete enough for downstream use.
STG-01 does not decide what the source means. That responsibility belongs to STG-02.
## 2. Separate reusable meaning from supporting context
STG-02 asks:
> What reusable semantic meaning and supporting context exist in the current accepted source material?
It creates candidates across the governed semantic layers:
- doctrinal authority;
- canonical capability meaning;
- opportunity meaning;
- enterprise context; and
- commercial application.
Evidence, examples, signals, use cases and explanatory material may also be retained as supporting context. Supporting context is useful for later retrieval, but it does not become semantic authority merely because it was extracted.
This separation prevents a useful example or screenshot from being mistaken for a canonical proposition, claim or capability definition.
STG-02 then validates candidates against bounded source evidence, compares compatible meanings and preserves distinctions such as equivalent, distinct, narrower, broader, conflicting, superseding or unresolved.
A structurally valid model response is still only a candidate. It is not automatically approved meaning.
## 3. Govern the connections
STG-03 asks:
> How are accepted current semantic objects validly connected?
A relationship is not created simply because two objects are similar, share a source or have compatible capability scope. The connection needs an approved relationship type, valid endpoint versions, the correct direction and sufficient support.
The resulting governed Relationship is distinct from its operational Edge:
- the Relationship is the governed semantic assertion;
- the Edge is its deterministic operational projection.
Supporting-context associations remain separate. They can connect evidence or examples to a semantic member for retrieval without becoming semantic authority, capability membership or ordinary graph traversal.
This distinction is central to trustworthy agentic work. A graph can be technically connected while still lacking sufficient evidence for a particular downstream use.
## 4. Assemble capability baselines
STG-04 asks:
> What governed semantic territory belongs to this capability, under which authority and boundaries?
It reads the current accepted database state from the STG-03 projection and assembles a purpose-neutral Capability Baseline.
The baseline preserves:
- one exact capability root;
- required canonical core meaning;
- applicable Layer 0 authority;
- supporting semantic territory;
- optional commercial references;
- separate supporting context;
- capability boundary constraints;
- explicit gaps; and
- exact membership-proof lineage.
Capability membership is therefore proven through version-pinned Relationship and Edge evidence. It is not inferred from similarity, source proximity, a scope label or an implementation document.
The baseline is not yet a communication profile. It is the governed territory that later Inspiral agentic-flow stages may use.
## Current implementation snapshot
The implementation closure recorded on 23 August 2026 produced the following bounded ContextCore result snapshot:
- 25 registered Source Hub records, with 24 in the normal default STG-01 denominator;
- 33 immutable STG-01 package or chunk materialisations, with semantic and supporting-context routes kept distinct;
- three completed capability baselines for Decision Integrity, Organizational Memory and Human–AI Accountability;
- governed semantic Relationships reconciled with their operational Edge projections; and
- separate supporting-context associations with lineage that can be inspected back to source evidence.
These figures are tied to the 23 August 2026 implementation closure and should be read together with the linked evidence surfaces. They are evidence of the completed current slice, not a claim that all organisational knowledge or every possible relationship has been captured.

## What “complete” means here
For the current governed ContextCore slice, completion means:
- the selected source and package denominator is accounted;
- returned semantic candidates are persisted or explicitly dispositioned;
- validation and comparison outcomes remain visible;
- accepted Relationships and operational Edges reconcile;
- supporting-context associations remain separate;
- capability membership has exact proof lineage;
- authority and boundary gaps are explicit;
- current results are distinguishable from historical results; and
- later stages can consume the baseline without rebuilding it from raw source prose.
Complete does not mean that every possible source, relationship or interpretation has been resolved.
## What ContextCore does not do
ContextCore does not:
- determine purpose-specific relevance;
- create a communication-purpose semantic profile;
- decide which meaning is worth communicating to a particular audience;
- generate an article or other communication asset;
- authorise a consequential state change; or
- prove that all organisational knowledge is complete or current.
In the Inspiral agentic flow, those responsibilities belong to later stages. CanonLens uses governed capability knowledge to derive contextual meaning; ContentFlight turns bounded meaning into validated communication.
## Why this matters for agentic systems
Without this foundation, each later task can quietly repeat the same dangerous shortcuts:
- rereading raw documents and producing a new interpretation;
- treating a plausible model response as authority;
- confusing similarity with a governed relationship;
- losing the evidence behind a claim;
- carrying forward superseded state; or
- inventing a missing boundary when context is incomplete.
ContextCore makes continuation safer because later agents can determine what information is current, where it came from, how it is connected and what they are allowed to do with it. That reduces the need to reread raw documents, recreate prior interpretations or silently widen the evidence boundary.
